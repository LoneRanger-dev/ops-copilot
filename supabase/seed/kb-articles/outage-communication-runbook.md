# Outage Communication Runbook

During a confirmed Sev-1 outage:

1. Declare the incident in the incident channel within 5 minutes of
   confirmation.
2. Page the on-call engineer for the affected service and the on-call
   incident commander.
3. Post a first customer-facing status update within 15 minutes, even if
   root cause is unknown — state impact and next update time, never
   speculate on cause publicly.
4. Update the status page every 30 minutes until resolved.
5. After resolution, schedule a blameless post-incident review within 3
   business days.
